## Brand & Style

PlantCare is a smart houseplant management mobile app designed to help urban dwellers cultivate thriving indoor gardens with ease. The brand personality is serene, intuitive, botanical, and deeply nurturing. It bridges the gap between technology and nature, providing calm clarity amidst the noise of daily life.

The visual style is grounded in **Minimalism** with organic warmth. It relies on generous whitespace, exceptionally crisp typography, understated structural lines, and a restrained color palette that evokes living foliage and morning dew. The interface feels weightless and breathing, putting the user's plants front and center without visual clutter.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from botanical environments, balancing deep forest tones with pale, breathable neutrals. 

- **Primary Green (`#2E7D4F`)**: Used for primary actions, active states, and key navigational elements.
- **Dark Green (`#1B4332`)**: Used for high-contrast typography, primary headlines, and anchoring structural elements.
- **Light Green (`#E8F5E9`)**: Used for subtle container backgrounds, success banners, and selected card states.
- **Sage Green (`#A8C3A0`)**: Used for secondary accents, icons, and muted indicators.
- **Background (`#F8FAF5`)**: The organic, off-white canvas tone that softens the interface.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean white for floating cards, sheets, and input fields.
- **Primary Text (`#26352B`)**: Deep charcoal-green for high-legibility body copy.
- **Secondary Text (`#788579`)**: Muted botanical grey-green for captions and metadata.
- **Border (`#E3EAE1`)**: Subtle divider and outline tone.
- **Error (`#D9534F`)**: Calibrated warning tone for alerts and urgent plant care needs.
